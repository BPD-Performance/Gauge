ECU trouble codes

Not linked yet

The Elite only reports and clears its trouble codes (DTCs) through OBD-II on its vehicle CAN bus. The gauge sits on the Haltech CAN bus, which runs at a different speed, so it can't reach them yet. Reading and clearing codes needs a second CAN connection on the gauge. Until then, use NSP to view and clear codes.

Dyno mode

Dyno mode

For the dyno, where the wheels turn but the car isn't going anywhere. Lets you save settings and keeps the gauge's WiFi on while the wheels are turning, with no 15 minute WiFi limit. The gauge shows DYNO at the bottom while it's on. It turns itself off when the gauge restarts.

Bench test

Makes the gauge generate realistic fake Haltech data (a 24 second loop: idle, a full-throttle run through 1st to 4th with boost, then idle). The gauge and this app behave as if the car is running. It switches off by itself on restart or if real ECU data appears, and keypad frames are never sent to the car while it runs. Boost + and − on the Keypad tab move the simulated target boost.
